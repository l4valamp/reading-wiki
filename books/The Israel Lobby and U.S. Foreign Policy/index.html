The Israel Lobby and U.S. Foreign Policy

Notes

This is one of the books I have been most frustrated about my lack-of-specific-recall for. I have gone back through summaries to try and log what I feel is most important.

Part 1

The United States, Israel, and the Lobby

  • Camp David Accords (1978) - Negotiated under Jimmy Carter between Menachem Begin and Anwar Sedat. Israel and Egypt peace.
  • US Support for Israel goes further than for other countries

Israel: Strategic Asset or Liability?

  • During Cold War, Israel allied with America against Soviet-backed Arab states
  • Six-Day War (1967)
    • Israel defeated Egyptian, Syrian, Jordinian forces
    • Occupied the West Bank, Gaza Strip, East Jerusalem, Sinai Peninsula, Golan Heights
    • Demonstrated Israel's military power.
  • Yom Kippur War (1973)
    • Egypt and Syria attacked Israeli positions
    • Richard Nixon authorized major American resupply operation
  • 1973 Oil Embargo
  • September 11 Attacks
  • Al-Qaeda and Osama bin Laden - American support for Israel was one of many grievances invoked by Laden.

A Dwindling Moral Cause

  • Balfour Declaration (1917) - British statement supporting a national home for the Jewish people in Palestine
  • British Mandate for Palestine - Period of British administration after World War I, during which tensions between Jewish and Arab communities intensified.
  • UN Partition Plan (1947) - Proposed dividing British Mandatory Palestine into Jewish and Arab states.
  • Nakba (1948) - Palestinian term meaning catastrophe, referring to the mass displacement and dispossession of Palestinians during the war surrounding Israel's creation.
  • 1948 Arab-Israeli War - Another term for the Nakba. Israel established independence, approximately 700,000 Palestinians fled or were expelled. Narrative that "Palestinians voluntarily abandoned their homes"
  • 1967 Occupation -

What is the "Israel Lobby" ?

  • AIPAC
  • ADL "Anti-Defamation League"
  • Christian Zionists
  • Neoconservatives - America world police, align with hard-line pro-Israel stances The book stresses that PACS and Lobbying are a part of the American democratic system and that Pro-Israeli groups Lobbying politicians is not the concern. Rather, the degree of influence and the resulting policies.

Dominating Public Discourse

Think tanks - WINEP (Washington Institute for Near East Policy) - Produces Middle East Policy research - Brookings Institution - Think tank. Middle East policy research. - Alan Dershowitz - Harvard law professor, prominent defender of Israel - Campus Watch project to scrutinize Middle East studies at universities. Book acknowledges antisemitism appearing in rhetoric about Israel, but argues that accusations of antisemitism can sometimes be used to discourage legitimate criticism of Israeli government policy.

Part 2: The Lobby in Action

The Lobby Versus the Palestinians.

  • Yasser Arafat
    • Chairman of the PLO (Palestine Liberation Organization)
    • Later President of the Palestinian Authority
    • Central figure in negotiations and Palestinian nationalism
  • Oslo Accords (1993)
    • Negotiated between Israel and the PLO
    • Created a framework for Palestinian self-government
    • Did not establish a Palestinian state or resolve final-status issues.
  • Yitzhak Rabin - Israeli prime minister who signed the Oslo Accords, assassinated in 1995 by an Israeli extremist opposed to the peace process.
  • Benjamin Netanyahu - Israeli prime minister 1996-1999, again beginning in 2009. Opposed important elements of the Oslo process.
  • Camp David Summit (2000)
    • Negotiations between Ehud Barak, Yasser Arafat, and Bill Clinton (failed).
    • The book argues against the narrative that Arafat failed, focused on key issues like Jerusalem, refugees, settlements, sovereignty, territorial continuity.
  • Second Intifada (2000-2005) - Period of intensified Israeli-Palestinian violence following collapse of peace process.
  • Hamas - Palestinian Islamist political and armed movement. Won the 2006 Palestinian legislative election. Book argues that American policymakers repeatedly accommodated Israeli positions, and undermined their own role as an impartial negotiator.

Iraq and Dreams of Transforming the Middle East

Book argues that some influential pro-Israel neocons promoted the invasion as part of broader Middle-East transformation strategy. It does not argue Israel or the lobby alone caused the war. Instead, that they were important advocates alongside other forces.

Book argues war promoted partly on assumption that removing Saddam would benefit Israel and facilitate broader regional transformations. Instead, produced instability, sectarian violence, increrased Iranian influence in Iraq.

  • Iraq War (2003) - American-led invasiton that overthrew Saddam Hussein.
  • George W. Bush authorized the invasion
  • Dick Cheney Vice pres. and lead advocate of confronting Iraq.
  • Donald Rumsfeld - Secratary of Defense
  • Paul Wolfowitz
    • Deputy Secretary of Defense
    • Prominent neocon and advocate of removing Saddam
    • Ambitious plans to reshape Middle Eastern politics
  • Richard Perle former defense official, prominent Neocon
  • William Kristol - Editor of The Weekly Standard, influential advocate of Iraq invasion
  • Clean Break Report (1996)
    • Policy paper prepared for incoming Israeli prime minister by a study group led by Perle
    • Advocated new Israeli regional strategy, including confroting Syria and challenging Saddam Hussein.

Taking Aim at Syria

  • Syria was an important regional adversary of Israel
  • Golan Heights - Syrian territory captured by Israel in 1967.
  • Hezbollah Lebanese Shiite political and armed organization supported by Syria and Iran, opposed to Israel
  • Clean break report included proposals for weakening Syria's regional position.
  • Neocons thought Iraq invasion would put pressure on Syria.
  • Syria Accountability and Lebanese Sovereignty Restoration Act (2003)
    • Authorized sanctions against Syria
    • Cited Syria's support for militant groups, presense in Lebanon, and alleged weapons program.
  • Cedar Revolution (2005) - Lebanese protests following Rafic Hariri's assasination, contributing to the withdrawl of Syrian troops from Lebanon.

Iran in the Crosshairs

  • Iranian Revolution (1979)
    • Overthrew US-backed Mohammad Reza Pahlavi (Shah)
    • Established the Islamic Republic under Ruhollah Khomeini
  • Iran Nuclear Program - Made America and Israel scared
  • Dual Containment - US strategy in 90s aimed at simultaneously containing Iraq and Iran.
  • Iran and Libya Sanctions Act of 1996 (1996) - US legislation imposed sanctions on Iranian and Libyan energy sectors.
  • 2003 Iranian Negotiation Proposal - Reported Iranian diplomatic overture through Switzerland. Seriousness debated.

The Lobby and the Second Lebanon War

  • Second Lebanon War (2006)
    • Conflict between Israel and Hezbollah.
    • Began after Hezbollah attacked an Israeli border patrol, killing and capturing soldiers.
  • Condoleezza Rice - US Sexratary of State during the war
  • Qana Airstrike (2006)
