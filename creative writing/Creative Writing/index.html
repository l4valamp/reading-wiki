SEASON 1

1. Pilot - Kidnapping

Requirements:

Unethical Mission: Selling water to a population that has been kept in an artificial drought Thematic Argument: Unethical behavior is rewarded by unethical systems Research Basis: Shock doctrine (taking advantage of disasters for business opportunity) Characters are challenged by: Berny is made to fill a position she finds demeaning, lie about mission to get ahead. Characters challenge each other by: Ed's plot puts Berny in a negative situation Buddy feels stifled by Berny's overprotectiveness Inciting Humiliation: Being given a job she feels insulted by Big, wrong decision: Lying to Pearl about Buddy being safe, after he has been kidnapped Temporary Benefits: Meeting with a recruiter from her dream crew Collapse: The recruiter realizes what's going on and threatens to ruin her Resolve: The recruiter is killed due to Buddy's incompetence, they lie about everything that happened to get ahead.

Skeleton:

PILOT- KIDNAPPING
ACT 1
New ATOMICORP graduate Berny walks into a seemingly empty ship. She is in awe until she sees a stranger lounging in the captains chair. She tells him this area is restricted, academy property. He doesn't seem to get it. His name is Buddy. She thinks he's a bum off the streets and begins insulting him and his handling of the ship. She explains she has spent her entire life going through rigorous training to command this vessel and her mentor was an ATOMICORP executive and was about to assign her to it. All of her hard work was about to pay off. He seems unaware of her hostility and thinks what she's saying is cool. Her mentor, Pearl, comes in. Berny code-switches and sweetly and professionally greets her. Buddy says "hi mom!" Berny is silently horrified at the realization this is her mentor's son, and horrified by how she spoke to him. He innocently parrots back some of her admonishments to Pearl (your filthy gloves shouldn't be anywhere near that console). Pearl brushes these off and hands him the keys to the ship. He’s the captain and she's excited to make Berny his second in command. Berny is horrified.
TITLE
Berny tries make peace with this situation while preparing Buddy for the mission. He's in an all-over face mask & robe spa getup. Buddy's manny, Ed, tries comforting her by telling her the simple joys of caring for Buddy, even if the pay isn't great. He also asks many logistical questions about the ship and shift schedule (overprotective, seemingly micromanagerial trait). but it just makes her frightened people will always consider her Buddy's nanny instead of a qualified fleet commander. She freaks out.
Berny asks Pearl what she did wrong and how she can get promoted. Pearl argues that this is the best use of her training. Berny politely insists she's not like the women who are trained to be stewardesses or caretakers. Pearl tells her that this job is a test run for her. Pearl knows Buddy is not the brightest, but Berny will be a good influence on him. She mentions how pleased she's been with Ed the past month he's been working for her. Pearl is sending them on a cushy mission to sell water and promote their new waterpark. If Berny just needs to keeps Buddy safe and out of trouble on this mission. He's a popular target for kidnappers looking for easy hostage money. If Berny can manage that, Pearl will arrange a meeting with an agent looking to take her.
ACT 2
Berny comes out of her meeting with a newfound determination to make sure nothing bad happens to Buddy. The mission begins. The crew is sent to sell water to displaced refugees of a planet-wide drought outside the gates of Atomicorp's new waterpark. Buddy wants to talk to and help the refugees, but Berny won't allow it. Buddy wants to give them water but Berny wont allow it unless they pay. Ed says he promised Buddy they could walk around the slums. Buddy says he's excited to be a do-gooder and take care of ruffians or bandits that could be about. but Berny vetoes this plan. A refugee says all of their money was taken by bandits. Berny does not care and instead asks for someone with money. She has hired a soldier, Trooper, who points a gun at potential new friends. Ed suggests Berny let Buddy have more of a role and tells him to talk to the next refugee in line. One refugee says he feels incoming heat stroke and needs a cooling pad. Buddy offers to let him in the ship to use his cooling spa contraption. Berny shuts this down and scares the refugee off, to Buddy's dismay. Ed says Berny should encourage Buddy's independence but Berny says all that matters to her is his safety. Buddy runs back into the ship, crying, and Ed goes after him. Another refugee comes up and says if they're still offering to take care of bandits, they've had a huge problem recently. Berny ignores him.
Back in the ship, Ed encourages Buddy not to listen to Berny. Buddy doesn't want to break her rules, but Ed points out how little she's letting him do, and how he's just as cooped up on the mission as he was at home. Ed says he managed to contact the refugee and invite him back onto the ship. Buddy agrees to use a back entrance to sneakily invite the refugee and his friends into the ship. Once they get on, they take off their rags, and start talking to Ed like this was pre-arranged. "Six way split?"
Berny voices her frustrations with this situation to Trooper, who accidentally ticks her off to an important realization that Ed is not who he says he is. Berny asks why Trooper didn’t join them. Trooper says Ed told her to stay with Berny. Berny is offended Trooper is listening to Ed instead of her, a commanding officer. Trooper says Ed showed a Vehicle command authority card. Berny realizes that’s incompatible with his tutor role. Just then, Ed runs back from the ship, yelling for help. Berny commands trooper to apprehend him instead. She gets trooper to search his pockets and a bunch of forged id cards drop out. She interrogates him about his real identity. He drops his act and gloats about it all, then smugly lets her know that she should be more worried that Buddy has just been kidnapped and is being held ransom. He says that this group can get rowdy, so if Berny is so concerned about Buddy's safety, she should really call Pearl and let her know about the emergency. His comm pad is already ringing.
Berny takes Ed's comm pad and responds to Pearl while Trooper muffles Ed. Before Berny can say anything, Pearl tells her she scheduled for the recruiter to visit them on the job. Ed is surprised when, instead of telling Pearl and calling in reinforcements, Berny collects herself and assures her that everything is going fantastic. Ed insists Berny is not going to be able to fix this situation herself, and his colleages won't give Buddy up without an armada of atomicorp drones, but Berny cites the manual on hostage situations and advanced interrogation methods. She orders Trooper to consult the page and get the information out of him through torture.
ACT 3
The recruiter has arrived and Berny tries to keep her from seeing that something has gone wrong. Makes excuses to keep her outside in the heat, uses up all of their water to keep her comfortable, pisses off the crowd of refugees. Berny moves her under the shade of the ship. The recruiter tells her about the job she's offering (Berny's dream job). She is very pleased with Berny at first but starts to get more and more annoyed. The refugee from earlier comes up from the crowd and thanks Berny for giving them the water and makes her look good in front of the recruiter but also takes the rest of the water.
Meanwhile, back by the ship, Trooper is trying to threaten/torture Ed for the code to get inside. Ed refuses to betray his colleagues in the face of pain. Trooper accidentally reveals Berny is authourizied to offer Ed a permanant contract and raise. Ed learns he would make more with his raise working for Buddy than he would make from ransoming Buddy. He immediately sells them out and gives Trooper the door keycode.
Buddy is wearing down the kidnappers and asking questions. He’s so excited to be on a real adventure. Trooper barges in and turns the situation immediately over the top and violent. In the chaos, Buddy tries to trigger the emergency hatch.
The recruiter sees the ship malfunctioning and hears the gunshots. She moves towards the ship, threatening to ruin Berny's career for letting things get this bad. Berny is on her knees begging to make things right. Then, the recruiter is killed by the emergency ship ladder Buddy accidentally deployed.
Berny is horrified, on her knees next to the dead recruiter when the crew comes down the escape hatch of the ship.
Hard cut to the crew recounting this story to Pearl/a council. They're asked to tell the full truth. Ed and Berny nod to each other. They say The kidnappers attacked the ship. Trooper eliminated them. Berny coordinated the rescue. The recruiter died in the crossfire. Buddy corroborates this as the best adventure he's had. They are given medals. The refugee who tried to warn them about bandits watches.
Button scene, Pearl congratulates Berny but holds off on the promotion reassessment for another year.

2. Military Base Feminism

Requirements:

Unethical Mission: The crew needs to either transport a singer or infiltrate a prostitution ring Thematic Argument: Don't let the boxes the military sorts women into prevent you from finding solidarity. Research Basis: Cynthia Enloe: Bananas, Beaches, and Bases. 12 Feminist lessons of War Characters are challenged by: - Berny wants the respect of established military generals but is lumped in to being seen as a prostitute. - Ed's pride is wounded when his past lover is revealed to be a prostitute - Trooper is humiliated by the guys she wants to be seen as one of Characters challenge each other by: - Trooper encourages the crew to exclude Berny for being such a girl - Berny annoys trooper by trying to cajole her into female solidarity - Berny earns the approval of Trooper's dad more than she ever could Inciting Humiliation: - Trooper gets the group to laugh at and exclude Berny - Ed's past love gives the general a lap dance Big, wrong decision: - Berny decides to humiliate Trooper - Buddy sleeps with the general's wife Temporary Benefits: - Berny gets to flaunt an obedient personal soldier - Buddy feels powerful and masculine Collapse: - The general finds out and attacks Buddy - Trooper fails to act in defense of the crew due to her shot ego Resolve: - Berny must embarrass herself to give Trooper her confidence back

Skeleton:

EPISODE - FEMINISM ON MILITARY BASES Q’s
ACT 1
The crew is told they're going to a military base. Berny is a big fan of the general, who is Trooper's dad. Trooper is also a big fan of her dad. Ed is dressed for vacation. He has an ex from here. (innocent as a lily flower), but she couldn't resist him. Does Berny want to join the general's crew?
TITLE
First impression of the general. Berny is horrified to realize this base is a prostitution hub. Trooper's dad does not care for her. Berny is professional. The guys want to pull him away for the night life. He hesitates, but Trooper puts the nail in the coffin by making fun of Berny for being a girl, and they go off. Trooper must be willing to throw down and fight at some point here.
Ed gets the general a lapdance. He realizes the woman he was smug about dating is actually a prostitute and is now dancing on this guy he's schmoozing. He's horrified. Buddy blurts out the story to the general, who laughs and rubs it in his face.
ACT 2
Berny confronts Trooper for embarrassing her. Tries to have a one on one "girl talk." waits in Trooper's room in the dark until she gets back to corner her. She says "women need to have each others backs. What's important is that neither of us are prostitutes." Trooper reasserts that Berny doesn't get it because she's a girl.
Buddy tries to comfort a sniffling Ed. Ed explains it’s not embarrassing for him to have slept with the prostitute, he just miscalculated the points he got from it. It's not impressive to have sex with a prostitute. He ranks the status of each role a woman has on the military base and how impressive it is to have sex with them numerically. Ed decides he needs to sleep with someone higher status on the base to impress the general. What does Ed want from the general?
Berny does something bad to prostitutes.
Some quick Ed and Buddy foreshadowing
Berny does something to disproportionately humiliate and immasculate Trooper. She gets Trooper's dad to say she's like the daughter he never had. What is this humiliation?
ACT 3
Berny is pleased that Trooper is being obedient in front of others and allowing her to social climb. What is the thing that happens?
It is revealed Buddy slept with a General’s wife and the general is trying to throw down. Trooper does not succeed in throwing down and instead cowers away.
Ed and Buddy yell at Berny to fix whatever is going on with Trooper. Berny thinks the prostitutes will cheer her up but they refuse to help her because of something she did to them earlier. What did berny do to the prostitutes?
Berny humiliates herself by dressing like one of the prostitutes on the base needing Trooper to rescue her. Trooper bounces back in confidence and gives a beatdown to whoever they needed her to beat down. who does trooper need to beat up? from earlier?

### 3. Lying To Ambassadors

Requirements:

Unethical Mission: The crew is sent to broker a business deal where they're trying to completely screw over a peaceful and honest sect of political ambassadors. Thematic Argument: Research Basis: Chinatown, Guatemala, east India company. https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/the-simpsons-marge-vs-the-monorail/ https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rules_of_Acquisition_%28Star_Trek%3A_Deep_Space_Nine%29 Characters are challenged by: Berny embarrasses herself by giving a totally honest and insulting speech to the ambassadors. Trooper must use words, not violence, to de-escalate a tense situation. Characters challenge each other by: Ed's demonstration of careful lying locks Buddy into a high stakes risky scam Buddy's insistence that everything can be handled through honesty makes Ed defensive Trooper takes advantage of Berny's honest state to get compliments and leverage out of her. Big, wrong decision: - Ed overpromies to the ambassador's leader, and then they're expected to deliver. - Trooper does not give Berny the antidote when she could. Temporary Benefits: The ambassadors forgives them for Berny's earlier transgression. Trooper gets juicier information from Berny, even vulnerability Collapse: The ambassadors now expects them to deliver on a claim Berny is unable to lie to back up their new scheme, so Trooper must work double time in her stead. Berny is furious with Trooper. Resolve: Buddy must lie and charm a monk, committing himself to higher stakes risk. Trooper must back up their scheme's claims despite being bad with words. They succeed but only after mutual embarrassment.

Skeleton:

EPISODE 3: LYING Q's
ACT 1
Berny is being fake nice to ambassadors that the crew finds repulsive. Trooper and Buddy are confused why they even deal with them. Ed explains theyre the only aliens with biology to synthesize the lava into gold. Buddy asks why they agree to such bad deals. Ed explains that Atomicorp lies that they have a machine that can do it, but really they just pay one of the aliens ungodly amounts of money. Buddy is aghast at this. Trooper wonders how hard it could be, and attempts it, but nearly dies.
TITLE
Ed and Berny have an argument in front of Buddy and Trooper. Ed calls Berny fake for pretending to like the ambassadors. Berny accuses Ed of lying the most out of all of them for personal gain.
Dinner with the ambassadors. Things are going astonishingly well. The ambassadors reveal they're catching on to ATOMICORP's tricks and fed them truth serum with their meals. Berny, as the only one who forced herself to eat the food, enters into a rage. She begins insulting them and revealing their plot to screw them over before Trooper knocks her out.
The ambassadors are furious and give the crew an ultimatum and a threat/consequence for failure. What is this ultimatum?
Ed and Buddy argue about how to handle this situation. Ed thinks they need to lie their way out of it. Buddy thinks they need to commit to total honesty. Ed wins out. Why does Ed win out?
Trooper hounds Berny with questions in the medical bay. What information does Trooper want from Berny? Establish earlier?
ACT 2
Ed tries to show Buddy how its done by lying to the leader of the ambassadors to get them out of trouble. This sates the leader. But then, Ed notices a new opportunity to push the lie slightly further to make some money off of the situation. The leader is delighted by his new lie and commits them to a next-day demonstration of their method working. What is this additional opportunity?
Trooper receives the antidote but does not give it to Berny. What more does Trooper want to hear from Berny? What does she do to the antidote so they cant use it later?
Buddy must lie to one of the ambassadors, and charm them, in order to get him to agree to fake this demonstration. This locks Buddy into a higher stakes commitment with that specific ambassador.
ACT 3
Ed, Buddy, and Trooper must complete some kind of ritual or ceremony or presentation to convince the ambassadors something is true. They must invent or admit to some kind of personal humiliation. This must be life or death for them. It works though
Buddy sends the ambassadors on their way, collecting individual donations from them as they file out. Ed asks Buddy if he sees how worthwhile his dishonest lifestyle is now. Buddy says he doesn't, they only gave him like 50 dollars. He gives Ed half. Ed blows up about how nobody has any integrity anymore. Buddy peels off, giggling, and counts the rest of the money he hid from Ed. He has learned the benefits of this lifestyle.
Trooper groans as Berny continues to be brutally honest and hyper-critical of her speech. She complains that the antidote might have been fake, Berny took it hours ago. Berny shrugs and smiles. "Weird, I guess it hasn't kicked in."

6. Space Whales

EPISODE - SPACE WHALE PROTESTORS (MAUDE)
ACT 1
The crew is assigned a mission to break up a protest in the mouth of a space whale. Buddy wants to win them over to liking his family. Berny says there’s no use trying to change their minds.
TITLE
The crew makes their initial, ill-advised attempt to get into the rebel base. Things go very wrong.
Berny wakes up and realizes she’s held captive by Maude, her highschool rival who ran away. Maude is moved to be seeing Berny and wants to reconnect, to the displeasure of her comrades and Berny. Maude tries To reason with Berny that she doesn’t have to kick them out and that the space whale mining is unethical.
Buddy asks leading questions in defense of his family. They’re answered by the reader who invites him to join the circle.
ACT 2
Maude tries using facts and logic to win Berny over. She doesn’t. She takes Berny to the whale’s brain stem and makes it communicate. Berny writes this off with a logical fallacy. Maude tries to correct her, and while she’s distracted, Berny gets her communicator back and gives the crew the route to the brain stem.
Buddy, who has become deeply entrenched in this Socratic seminar, realizes they’re all reading the Luna-bomber manifesto and seems horrified.
Maude tries using emotional arguments to win Berny over. This seems to maybe be making some ground with her.
Buddy runs out of the camp and bombs the mining rig, causing a chain reaction and the whale starting to swallow the camp.
ACT 3
The whale is swallowing them. Maude cuffs Berny to herself to rescue her. (To the protests of her comrades) but Berny uses the opportunity to turn on Maude, despite their MAD. This breaks Maude’s heart.
The crew is asked about the bombing. Buddy immediately blames the protestors.
Berny is pleased despite being burnt up. Maude’s body is taken by the company.
