Military Base

Requirements:

Mission
The crew is assigned to chaperone a famous performer to sing at an Atomicorp military base on her home planet. They're horrified to discover a massive prostitution hub has sprouted up around the base.
Comic Premise
Berny and Trooper try to win a general's approval by positioning their own conflicting brands of femininity as setting them above "other women".
Character Wants
Berny wants the general's approval and to be seen as respectable
Trooper wants the general's approval and to be seen as one of the guys.
Big Wrong Decision:
Trooper humiliates Berny by lumping her in with the prostitutes.
Berny humiliates Trooper by lumping her in with the maids.
Comic Irony
They try to gain approval as women by tearing down other women and reinforcing the patriarchal structures keeping them down.
Ending Condition
Berny flirts with Trooper to get Trooper to beat up the rational singer. They haven't achieved solidarity, but instead found a way to feed into each others' unhealthy self image.

Research:

Bananas, Beaches, Bases. Cynthia Enloe.

Skeleton:

TITLE
They’re transporting a classy singer to a military base for a morale performance on her home planet. What’s the cold open gag?
ACT 1
TITLE
They get there and it’s a prostitution hub. The singer is horrified because of the unethical economic and safety conditions of the workers. Berny is horrified because she’s getting lumped in with the prostitutes (maybe this is also her home planet?) the general respects that not everyone from that race is promiscuous, but Trooper makes fun of an intentional choice Berny made to appear more presentable and feminine to disregard her and lump her in. Berny insults the showgirls and the performer claims her.
Berny confronts trooper in her room- ambush. Says what matters is that neither of them are prostitutes. Trooper rebuffs her.
Berny assigns Trooper to be laundry for the showgirls. Trooper feels demeaned and grossed out. Then when they start undressing conflicted and confused.
ACT 2
Berny tries to get the starlet to perform but she won’t because of the treatment or pay of the sex workers.
Berny tries to prompt Trooper to act in an important moment and she won’t because she feels dressed up.
