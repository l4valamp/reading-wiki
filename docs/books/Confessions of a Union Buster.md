# Confessions of a Union Buster

## Notes

Super interesting book! Used as inspiration for a writing project. 

Some tactics he mentioned:

**Divide and Conquer:** Highlight differences of interest between groups of workers. Men vs women, day shift vs night shift, young vs old, etc. 

**Fixing some problems on behalf of management:** Getting rid of core gripes to take steam away from the workers and make it clear that management is capable of solving problems without the union.

**Choosing mouthpieces from management:** Since managers are barred from union membership, they can be held in specific anti-union training. Selecting for well-respected and senior staff, potentially dangling promotion or turning workers against each other.

**Walking the floor:** One of the first steps, getting a feel for the office and pin-pointing popular workers and potential tensions. 

**Captive Audience Meetings:** Mandatory anti-union meetings for workers. 

**Barrage of emails:** Blowing union powers/information privileges to fear monger (EX: The union has a list of all of your emails and addresses!)
