# A Collective Bargain

## Notes

Read after [[Confessions of a Union Buster]]. Interesting details on the [[L.A. Teachers' union]]. Specifically giving up pay for more green spaces for kids, combats arguments that teachers unions are greedy. Talks about how higher paid/skilled teachers are fired to avoid paying benefits and high turnover.

Also talks about nursing. Some nurses being turned against each other by union busters. 

Also talks about the strategy of bringing in established unions and their representatives to start one's own union. Includes union busting tactics at the end of the book.
