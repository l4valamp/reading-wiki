# Between Two Fires

## Notes

I really enjoyed between two fires. I liked how the prose was readable but didn't feel too modern. I like that they kill the nice priest and then avoided the bury-your-gays trope by revealing his brother, who's both evil and significantly gayer. 

Recommended this book to a friend's dad, who didn't get through the first chapter because of the impending rape, which is frustrating because the rapists are all immediately gruesomely killed before they can do anything in the page after he quit. It's nice how averse he was to it, though.
