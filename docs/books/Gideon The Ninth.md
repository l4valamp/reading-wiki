# Gideon The Ninth

## Notes

I totally called that Tamsyn Muir was a Homestuck fan even when going in blind. I think they used the words "Absconded" or "Umbral Maiden" somewhere in the first few chapters and I locked on. 

I started reading this because of Ianthe a billowy button down in comics online and it did not disappoint on that front. 

A guy named john and his friends destroy the universe in a flash of fiery apocalyptic glory and are then tasked with working together to gain powers to create a new universe, staining their image on everything to come after. Many of them die in the pursuit of going "God-tier". Many of them fuse. They have skeletal denizens. They are on Tumblr. Eridan is there. It's also DaveRose, which is weird and I feel complicated about.
